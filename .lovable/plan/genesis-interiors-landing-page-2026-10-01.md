# Genesis Interiors landing page

## Experience
- Build one immersive, mobile-ready landing page at `/` for superyacht owners, naval architects, estate developers, and interior designers.
- Follow the selected **Frosted Marble Light** direction: Cormorant Garamond and Jost, an ivory / deep ink / muted gold palette, delicate translucent surfaces, generous editorial spacing, restrained reveals, and a prominent opening statement. Refine its large rounded cards and ambient effects into a more architectural, image-led luxury presentation without changing its section order or primary composition.
- Show the Genesis Interiors name, Yacht & Residence positioning, Florida–Italy footprint, portfolio path, and private consultation action immediately. Keep the next section visible near the opening viewport.

## Content and conversion
- Present yacht interiors and refits, residential millwork, bespoke furniture, and full-scale in-house pre-assembly; explain the 22,000-square-foot workshop and patented Fit-Lock® system in concrete, verifiable language.
- Showcase selected yacht and residential projects with accurate names, dimensions, years, and collaborators from the supplied brief. Include a browsable, grouped master yacht project list without making the main page read like a spreadsheet. Distinguish confidential work and future-dated projects rather than implying they are complete.
- End with a prominent **private project inquiry** for an engineering feasibility and custom 3D mockup *consultation*, capturing contact details and the vessel/property brief. The form should submit to persistent storage and show clear success/error feedback; offer the headquarters phone and email as direct alternatives. Do not assert a free study, a guaranteed mockup, reserved slot, fixed response time, or legal confidentiality/precision guarantees unless those terms are provided by Genesis.
- Include official address, social links, and Genesis Group links. Add distinctive page metadata and accessible navigation.

## Technical approach
- Replace the starter page using the existing TanStack Start/Tailwind system. Add semantic design tokens and reusable button styling; make navigation, project browsing, and inquiry controls work on desktop and mobile.
- Prefer accurately identified photographs from the official Genesis site for named work, stored as project assets rather than hotlinked. Use supporting generated imagery only where it cannot be mistaken for a documented project.
- Enable Lovable Cloud for the inquiry submissions, create a narrowly scoped submissions table with appropriate grants and access policies, and process validated submissions server-side. Do not expose submitted briefs publicly. Without a calendar or email-sending service, the inquiry is a request, not a booked appointment or an email delivery guarantee.
- Verify the page visually at desktop and mobile sizes and submit a real test inquiry through the page, confirming its success and stored result.
